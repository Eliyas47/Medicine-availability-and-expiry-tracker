# 💊 Medicine Availability & Expiry Tracker

**A digital solution for smarter pharmacy inventory management and medicine availability tracking.**

The Medicine Availability & Expiry Tracker aims to simplify how pharmacies monitor their medicine stock, manage expiry dates, and identify products that need replenishment. By bringing essential inventory information into one platform, the project seeks to reduce manual work, improve stock visibility, and support more efficient pharmacy operations.

## 🌟 Why This Project?

Effective medicine inventory management is important for maintaining reliable stock records and minimizing preventable waste. Manual tracking can make it difficult to identify expiring batches, anticipate shortages, and quickly determine where a particular medicine may be available.

This project proposes a centralized platform that helps pharmacy staff manage inventory while providing users with access to recently reported medicine availability information.

## 🎯 What the Platform Offers

### 🏪 Centralized Inventory Control
- Create and maintain medicine records.
- Manage quantities, categories, and batch information.
- Record incoming and outgoing stock.
- Search and filter inventory efficiently.

### 📅 Expiry Management
- Monitor expiry dates for individual medicine batches.
- Identify products approaching expiration.
- Separate expired stock from usable inventory.
- Generate notifications based on configurable expiry periods.

### 🚨 Intelligent Stock Alerts
- Set minimum inventory thresholds.
- Identify medicines that require replenishment.
- Highlight products with insufficient or zero stock.
- Help pharmacy staff plan restocking activities.

### 🔍 Medicine Availability Discovery
- Search for medicines by name.
- Find participating pharmacies reporting availability.
- View pharmacy contact details and location.
- Check when availability information was last updated.

### 📈 Inventory Reporting
- Review current stock levels.
- Identify low-stock and expired batches.
- Monitor inventory changes over time.
- Generate reports to support purchasing decisions.

### 🔐 Secure Access
- Authenticate users securely.
- Apply role-based permissions.
- Restrict pharmacy inventory to authorized users.
- Maintain records of important inventory changes.

## 🌍 Vision and Expected Benefits

The project is designed to explore how software can help address practical healthcare inventory challenges in Ethiopia.

Potential benefits include:
- Better visibility into medicine stock.
- Earlier identification of inventory shortages.
- Improved monitoring of approaching expiry dates.
- Reduced avoidable medicine waste.
- Easier discovery of pharmacies reporting medicine availability.

These benefits will need to be evaluated through implementation and real-world testing.

## ⚠️ Responsible Use

This application is intended for inventory management and availability information. It is not a medical diagnosis or prescribing tool. Expired medicines must never be shown as available stock, and public listings should display their latest confirmation time. Deployment should comply with applicable Ethiopian pharmaceutical and data-protection requirements.
